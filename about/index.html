Who We Are

An Independent Digital Platform

JiScroL is an independent digital platform dedicated to curating and delivering timely updates, entertainment highlights, sports and cultural insights, with a particular focus on music, trends, and everyday developments for a Ugandan audience.

Built for people who value both relevance and quality, JiScroL is designed to keep you informed and engaged without the noise. Every piece of content is selected and presented with clarity and intentionality.

Our Approach

Content With Purpose

JiScroL operates as a news aggregation and content curation platform. Articles, updates, and media highlights are sourced from credible third parties and summarised for informational purposes. Where applicable, original source links are provided for readers who wish to explore further.

This platform is independently managed and continuously improved. Content standards, design, and delivery are regularly refined to maintain a high-quality experience.

External Data

Data & Services We Rely On

Some of what you see on JiScroL comes from outside providers. We fetch it through our own servers and display it on the platform. We do not create or own it.

  • Sports data. Fixtures, scores, results and league tables come from a third-party football data provider. Scores may be delayed and are not guaranteed to be live or complete. Club names, crests and logos belong to their respective owners and are shown for identification only.
  • News. Headlines, short descriptions, images and links come from a third-party news aggregation service and the publishers behind it. Each item links back to its original source, and the publisher keeps all rights in it.
  • Hosting & infrastructure. The platform runs on third-party cloud services that host our website and servers, store our database, and may deliver and protect traffic. These providers process technical data (such as your IP address) as part of keeping the platform running.
  • Fonts. Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers when a page loads.
  • Link previews. When a JiScroL link is shared on a platform such as X, Telegram or WhatsApp, that platform fetches the page title, description and image to build a preview.

Because of these dependencies, parts of the platform may occasionally be slow, outdated or unavailable through no fault of our own. We cache some external data to keep the platform fast and to stay within provider limits. Data you see may therefore be up to a few hours old. Data we receive is used only to operate and display the platform, and we do not use it to profile you.

Privacy

Privacy & Your Data

We keep data collection to what the platform needs to work. Here is what that means in practice:

  • What you give us. Ratings, votes and opinions you submit, together with the name or username shown beside them.
  • What is collected automatically. Our hosting providers keep standard server logs, which may include your IP address, device and browser type, pages requested and timestamps. These are used for security, troubleshooting and measuring load.
  • How we use it. To display your contributions, prevent abuse and spam, keep the platform running, and improve it.
  • What we don't do. We do not sell your personal information.
  • Sharing. Data is shared only with the service providers that run the platform, or where the law requires it.
  • Retention & deletion. We keep contributions for as long as the platform operates or until removal is requested. To have your contributions or related data removed, email us at the address below.
  • Your rights. You may ask what data we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion, in line with the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 of Uganda and any other law that applies to you.
  • Children. JiScroL is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

No online service is completely secure. We take reasonable steps to protect data, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Cookies & Storage

Cookies & Local Storage

JiScroL may store small pieces of information in your browser, for example your light or dark mode choice or whether you have already voted on a post, so the platform remembers you between visits. This stays on your device and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.
